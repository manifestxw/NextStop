---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the owner about an unresolved product or design decision when they request a plan review.
---

Read the proposal and the relevant project decisions. Resolve questions from those files first.
Choose the open assumption that most affects the demo, rider experience, or money flow.
Ask one concrete question at a time. Where several answers are viable, give the owner the
tradeoff and your recommendation. Use each answer to narrow the next question.
Finish when the plan is actionable or the owner ends the interview. Summarize the choices
made and any question that still needs evidence.
